ERP & SYSCOHADASYSCOHADA at the heart of your ERP+

A financial ERP should connect purchases, sales, payments and accounting. For Muhimu, revised SYSCOHADA accounting belongs in this foundation: it shapes configuration and operational workflows.

Start with your processes

Identify supporting documents, approval owners and reporting needs to define the project. Your accounting team should validate the chart of accounts, journals and processing rules.

Build a traceable workflow

Linking operations to their supporting documents and making approvals visible helps teams follow the data. Test against your actual business scenarios before launch.

INVOICING & TREASURYFrom invoice to payment+

Issuing an invoice and collecting its payment are different stages. Your system should help teams track due dates, payments and outstanding balances, with enough context to act.

Track the stages that matter

Define invoice statuses, payment terms and follow-up responsibilities so that information stays connected across teams.

Connect your tools

Connections between invoicing, treasury and accounting depend on your systems and payment channels. Assess feasibility and constraints during project discovery.

FINANCIAL REPORTINGFrom data to decisions+

A dashboard is useful when every indicator has a clear definition. Before designing charts, specify the data sources, reporting period and people who will use the results.

Choose useful questions

Which invoices are coming due? What amounts remain outstanding? Which differences need investigation? Business questions should guide your indicators.

Make the numbers explainable

Each figure should be traceable to its source. Access permissions, refresh frequency and consistency checks belong in the reporting project.
